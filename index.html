Every machine on peaq, drawn to scale.

Machine Elevations turns a machine's peaqID and credit record into an architectural front elevation. The same ID always gives the same drawing. The drawing changes only when the machine's record changes: its rating, its activity, its bond.

Add credit data from peaq
  1. Find a machine at app.peaq.xyz/machines and copy its DID into Machine ID.
  2. Open its record in the MCR API.
  3. Copy the JSON, paste it here, then press Apply. You only need this if the live record didn't load.

Sheet 02 · Finish schedule

The finish is the credit rating

A machine is drafted on plain paper until peaqOS rates it. As its Machine Credit Rating rises, the same drawing is reprinted in a richer finish, from field print to approved blueprint to gilded. Here is Woon at each stage.

Sheet 03 · Body system

The peaq series

The demo borrows bodies from GRID_ARCHITECT_SERIES. The production version gets its own body system, drawn only for peaq: modular heads, hulls, drives and tools in peaq's white, indigo and cyan, matched to each machine type. These are the first four prototypes. Machine types already get their own body: sensors are drawn as the PQ Listener, humanoids as the PQ Operator and claw machines as the PQ Arcade.

Sheet 05 · Legend

Drawing legend

Every mark on the sheet comes from the machine's record. Nothing is random at draw time.

peaqID
Hashed into a seed that picks the body, the name and the rhythm of blinking and breathing.
Operator
Sets the drafting grid, so a whole fleet is drawn on the same paper.
Credit rating
The finish: Drafting Paper until rated, then Field, Night Shift, Blueprint and Gilded.
Events
How alive the core is: pulse, glow and scanner speed. No events, no scanner.
Bond
Bonded machines breathe and blink. Unbonded machines are greyed out with their eyes shut.
Negative flag
A redline revision stamp: flagged, under review.
Revenue trend
Recorded in the title block as up, stable or down.
Home chain
peaq or Solana, recorded in the title block.
